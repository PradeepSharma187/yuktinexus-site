Corrections and nuances

  • Issuer-sponsored over third-party? Partly right. The staff taxonomy covers both and says each is a security. The Innovation Exemption admits third-party tokens with the same rights, gives issuers a 30-day objection right and excludes synthetics. The issuer-only stance is the transfer agents' petition, not SEC policy [S4][S8][S23][S25].
  • "ATS venues clear digital NMS stocks"? The order creates TSVs that are neither exchanges nor ATSs, and it concerns trading, not clearing. Clearing for Nasdaq-routed tokenized trades still goes through NSCC and DTC [S4][S16].
  • Not re-verified in this pass: the 4 Nov 2026 first TSV trade, Canton Coin no-action relief, some NYSE participant lists, Avalanche and Solana credit deployments and one repo volume figure. Global sections of the earlier report are out of scope.

Open items and what to watch

  • Tokenized IPO: no issuer or date named, so the onchain primary market is untested [S30].
  • Fee transparency: DTC tokenization pricing, TSV fees and tokenized custody costs were not found in any source.
  • Scale: 34 eligible securities and 20 participants against a $114T depository [S10].
  • Exemption durability: five-year limit, issuer objection, symbol caps with no grace period, comments invited [S2][S5].
  • Rule 611 outcome, final transfer agent rules, NYSE platform approval, GENIUS effectiveness by 18 Jan 2027, the Fed payment account and the Collateral AppChain (Q1 2027).
  • Tax form resolution before 2026 returns are filed in 2027 [S58].
