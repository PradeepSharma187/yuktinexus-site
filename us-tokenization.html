Vocabulary

TermMeaning
DTC entitlement token (Model A)A token representing the participant's securities entitlement held at DTC, one for one, convertible both ways [S9].
Issuer-sponsored token (Model B)The blockchain is the master securityholder file, or a token drives updates to an offchain file kept by the transfer agent [S22].
Third-party custodial token (Model C)An unaffiliated party mints tokens one for one against shares held in the regulated custody chain, as in Ondo's US launch [S28].
Synthetic token (Model D)The third party's own instrument, such as a loan note or swap, tracking a price. No ownership or vote. Outside the Innovation Exemption [S4].
Master securityholder fileThe issuer's official record of who owns what, kept by the issuer or its transfer agent [S21].
TSV (tokenized securities venue)A permissioned venue using AMM liquidity pools under the Innovation Exemption. Neither an exchange nor an ATS [S4].
Covered FirmA liquidity provider exempt from dealer registration only for TSV pool activity, with conditions [S7].
DvP and atomic settlementDelivery versus payment: security and cash move together. Atomic means in one indivisible step.
Deposit tokenA token that is a claim on a bank deposit, for example JPM Coin [S42].
Permitted payment stablecoinA stablecoin issued under the GENIUS Act regime [S44].
